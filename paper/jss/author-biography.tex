\textbf{Journal:} Journal of Systems and Software

\section*{Biography (long form, 123 words: EXCEEDS the JSS 100-word limit, kept for reference)}

Mateusz Miotk is an assistant in the Division of Combinatorial Optimisation, Institute of Informatics, Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Informatics, University of Gdańsk, Poland, where he holds an M.Sc. His earlier work is in structural and algorithmic graph theory: disjoint dominating and 2-dominating sets, graphs with equal domination and covering numbers, bipartization, and spanning trees with disjoint dominating sets, published in Discrete Optimization, the Journal of Combinatorial Optimization, Graphs and Combinatorics, and Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory, and more recently on constructing certified dominating sets in social networks. His current research applies the same concern with exact, checkable statements to the empirical performance measurement of server-side software: benchmark comparability, measurement validity, and reproducible experimental protocols for database access layers in Node.js services. ORCID: 0000-0003-2768-7861.
